## Deposits

### Flint Deposit

- 4-5 per layer
- Reserves of the deposit: 50-70 flint pieces
- Mining station: Clay Drill
### Coal Deposit

- 5-7 per layer
- Reserves of the deposit: 100-150 flint pieces
- Mining station: Flint Drill
### Copper Deposit

- 4-5 per layer
- Reserves of the deposit: 60-80 copper ore
- Mining station: Copper Drill
## Tools

### Flint Pickaxe

- Basically a rock taped to a stick. Humanity’s first invention, and still the absolute best way to ruin your wrists.
- Recipe: 5 flint pieces + 10 sticks
- Can dig through: cobblestone, flint, coal, copper
- Damage: 6 HP
### Copper Pickaxe

- We upgraded from rocks to metal! Now your wrists hurt faster.
- Recipe: 5 copper ores + 10 sticks
- Can dig through: cobblestone, flint, coal, copper
- Damage: 10 HP
### Mini copper drill

- A handheld power tool. Perfect for mining, terrible for hugs.
- Recipe: 25 copper ores + 20 flint pieces
- Can dig through: cobblestone, flint, coal, copper
- Damage: 20 HP
## Mining Stations

### Clay drill

- Made of soft clay, but aggressively punches hard flint. Physics is just a suggestion down here.
- Recipe: 25 clay pieces + 20 stones
- Extracts flint pieces
- Mining speed: 5 flint pieces/minute
### Flint drill

- Built from sharp flint to dig up dirty coal. It’s basically a geologist with anger issues.
- Recipe: 20 flint pieces + 20 stones
- Extracts coal pieces
- Mining speed: 7 coal pieces/minute
### Copper drill

- A copper machine that mines copper. It’s an industrial ouroboros. Very efficient, slightly depressing.
- Recipe: 10 copper ores + 15 flint pieces
- Extracts copper ore
- Mining speed: 4 copper ore/minute
