## Surface layer
is the first game level. Its depth ranges from 0 to 100 meters. It consist of **2 block** types: **dirt** and **rock**.
### Dirt resourcers and droppping probabilites
- stick - 40%
- stones - 20%
- seedling - 10%
- сlay - 30 %
### Rock resourcers and droppping probabilites
- cobblestone - 50%
- stones - 30 %
- nothing - 20%
### Tools
- paws, decreases block's HP by 1
- wooden shovel, recipe: 12 sticks, decreases block's HP by 2
- stone pickaxe, recipe: 5 sticks + 5 stones, decreases block's HP by 4
