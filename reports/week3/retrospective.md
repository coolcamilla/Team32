## What went well

1. **Work was divided effectively across roles.** Programmers, artist, and the game designer worked on their respective tasks in parallel without significant overlap or confusion about responsibilities.
2. **All Sprint PBIs were completed on time.** Full MVP v1 scope was delivered by the submission deadline without needing to cut any planned features.
3. **Sprint review provided valuable insight.** The Sprint Review with the customer helped the team identify both the strengths of the current build and areas that need improvement, such as the missing sense of risk in the gameplay loop.

## What did not go well

1. **Communication between team members was insufficient.** The team lead was often unaware of which tasks programmers were actively working on, making it difficult to track progress and identify blockers early.
2. **PBI preparation and estimation took too long.** Significant time was spent refining backlog items before the Sprint could begin, which delayed the start of implementation.
3. **Team members had different visions for the game content.** Misalignments in expectations about game mechanics and scope led to misunderstandings that required additional discussion to resolve.

## Action points

1. **Introduce a brief daily sync.** Each team member posts a short update on their current tasks in the team chat at the start of each working day so the team lead and other members stay informed without needing separate check-ins.
2. **Begin PBI refinement earlier.** Start refining backlog items for the next Sprint during the current Sprint rather than leaving it entirely to Sprint Planning.
