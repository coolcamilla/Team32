## Learning Points
- user story should be divided into supporting PBIs if and only if each of these PBI requires its own implementer and/or acceptance criteria
- sum of story points of supporting PBIs approximately equals to the story points of their parent user story
- dependencies between PBIs need to be identified explicitly during refinement so that implementers know the expected order of work
- there is no need to overcomplicate the game and add a lot of mechanics into it
- our goal is to produce a complete and fulfilling game, not a complicated one
## Validated assumptions
- We assumed that drag-and-drop items reallocation would be useful for users - rejected during the customer meeting
- We assumed that basic WASD and Space movement was sufficient - rejected during the customer meeting
- We assumed that the core gameplay loop should involve drill upgrading - confirmed after testing in MVP v1
- We assumed that automatization in the form of mining stations should be added to the game - confirmed during the customer meeting
- We assumed that lighting mechanic using a flashlight would interest players - confirmed during the customer meeting
## Friction and gaps
- risk mechanic is missed (something that motivates players to upgrade)
- unique movement mechanics for the mole
- number of layers (3 or 6)
- types and number of accessories for the mole
- core gameplay loop feels boring
